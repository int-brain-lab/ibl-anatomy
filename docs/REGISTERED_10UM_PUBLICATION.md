## Publication boundary

`ibl-anatomy` will publish one immutable lock pointing at a durable root projection manifest and
its complete transitive resource graph. The lock will retain the exact parent/display/projection
relationship above, record source terms and citations, and add no browser cache or renderer state.
The existing Python registered-projection reader remains the semantic validator.

The optional 10 um scalar intensity-block transport is a distinct asset and publication decision.
It may share the grid and reference space but is not part of this registered-SVG lock and must not
change Ephys Atlas display sampling.

## Unresolved publication inputs

The following are required before a public lock can be committed:

1. authoritative source URLs/object identities for the Allen annotation and bilateral LUT;
2. the applicable Allen terms, attribution, and citation record for redistribution of the derived
   registered geometry;
3. confirmation that both the sparse 80 um display derivative and projection root belong in the
   shared publication rather than remaining Ephys deployment products; and
4. an approved immutable origin whose served bytes, CORS headers, and opaque-gzip behavior can be
   tested.

Until those inputs are resolved, the hashes above are candidate source evidence only. Consumers
must not redirect production URLs to a local checkout or mutable branch.
